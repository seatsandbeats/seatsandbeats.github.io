# Seats & Beats Support

## Help is in the app

Every screen in Seats & Beats has a **?** button that opens Help for that screen. The full guide is under **Settings**, then **Help**. It covers setting up your riders, stops and runs, the seating chart, song requests and playing music on the bus.

You can also read the whole [Driver's Guide](guide) here.

## Common questions

**Do I need Apple Music?** To play music, yes: an Apple Music subscription on your iPhone. The seating chart works without one.

**Where is my data?** On your iPhone and in your own private iCloud. Nobody else can see it, including the developer. See the [privacy policy](privacy).

**I got a new iPhone.** Sign in with the same Apple Account and turn on iCloud for Seats & Beats. Your riders, charts and songs come back by themselves.

**Something isn't working.** In the app, open **Settings**, then **Share a support file**, and send it to the address below with a short note about what happened. A support file has no riders' names in it.
