# Seats & Beats Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-13

Seats & Beats collects no data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer cannot see anything you enter.

## Who uses the app

Seats & Beats is for school bus drivers. Riders do not use it and have no accounts. What a driver enters about riders (for example a first name, a grade, a stop and the songs they asked for) is entered by the driver and stays with the driver, as described below.

## What stays where

- Riders, stops, songs, driver's picks, seating charts and play history are stored on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud (CloudKit) account. The developer cannot see this data.
- Riders' marks, notes, incidents, rewards and bus card details (including a last name or student number, if you choose to type one) are stored the same way: on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud.
- Your preferences (crossfade, automatic playlist updates and the other settings) follow you to your other devices through your own iCloud. The developer cannot see them. The day's playback position is kept only on the iPhone.
- Nothing is shared with the developer, advertisers or any other third party.
- A report, a summary, a seating chart or an incident summary contains names and your own words. It leaves your iPhone only when you share or copy it. Text you copy in the app stays on that iPhone and is cleared after ten minutes.
- A support file (Settings, then Share a support file) leaves your iPhone only when you share it. It contains no names and none of the words you typed.

## Apple Music

Seats & Beats asks for access to Apple Music only to search for songs, play them, preview them, and keep your bus playlists up to date. To save cellular data it reads your own bus playlists in your Apple Music library, so it can play the songs you downloaded there. An Apple Music subscription is required to play music. Playback and playlists are handled by Apple under Apple's own terms.

## Photos

If you ask it to, Seats & Beats saves its playlist cover images to your photo library. It only adds images and never reads your photos.

## Location

Seats & Beats uses your location only if you turn on a GPS test drive, and then only while music plays on that one day. The test is optional and off unless you turn it on; iPhone asks for your permission ("While Using the App") when you do. It keeps times and measurements (when the bus stood still, how exact the position was, the battery level), never where you were: no location is saved on the phone, in iCloud or anywhere else. Nothing leaves your iPhone unless you share a support file, which has no locations in it.

If a future version adds optional stop locations, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

## Deleting your data

Settings, then Reset, then "Erase all data" deletes everything Seats & Beats stores, including its copy in your iCloud. Deleting the app removes what is on the iPhone.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
