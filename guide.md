# Seats & Beats — Driver's Guide

Seats & Beats is an iPhone app for school bus drivers. Each rider has a list of songs, and Seats & Beats plays one a day, taking turns. It builds an Apple Music playlist for each weekday in bus-stop order, so each rider hears their song while riding. It plays them from the **Now Playing** screen, keeps the rotation fair, keeps your bus's seating chart, and backs everything up to your private iCloud.

Only you use the app. The five tabs are **Week**, **Riders**, **Seats**, **Songs** and **Route**. **Settings** is the gear at the top right of every tab.

## Setting up

Do this once, before your first route. It takes about half an hour, plus the time to choose songs.

### Getting started

Seats & Beats turns song requests into five Apple Music playlists, one per weekday, in stop order.

On the **Week** tab, follow the four steps of **Get the bus ready**:

- **Set up your route.** The **Route** tab starts with a morning and an afternoon run. To add one, tap **+**. See **Runs, schools and the layover**.
- **Add riders.** Open the **Riders** tab and tap **+**. Pick each rider's school (it decides their run), stop and grade; **Add and start another** saves and opens the next.
- **Pick songs.** Open a rider's page and add songs, up to 10 (change it in **Settings**). One plays a day, in turn.
- **Your playlists are made automatically** once a rider has a song.

On the **Riders** tab, riders are listed **A to Z** with their stop and time. Tap **Sort** (top right) and choose **By stop** to group by stop; it remembers. Riders who need a school or a stop stay at the top. A button for each run shows only its riders, and search finds everyone, with other runs' riders under **In other runs**. In **By stop**, riders on a run with no requests are under **No stop**.

The **Songs** tab lists every requested song and its days.

Apple Music must be connected: if you see **Connect Apple Music**, tap **Connect**.

Want to look around first? Before setting up your route, tap **Or try it with a sample route and riders**; remove it later in **Settings**.

### How the plan works

Seats & Beats counts time from the moment you press play. Each run on the **Route** tab has its own times, and each stop has its time on that run.

It places each rider's song after they get on and before they get off, if you stay near your usual schedule. A rider who gets off at the first school hears their song before that school's release.

**A morning run and its afternoon run are planned as one list.** When the same riders ride a morning run and an afternoon run, Seats & Beats joins the two: a rider's song plays on whichever has room. Any other run is planned on its own. A **Driver's picks** or **No music** run plays no riders' songs; adding a **No music** run leaves your other runs' music as it is. With a **Driver's picks** run the same riders hear the same songs, but picks are shared, so which play, and whether a song plays morning or afternoon, can shift.

The **Safety margin** keeps slack. With 1 minute, a song starts at least 1 minute after the pickup and ends at least 1 minute before the drop-off or release. Set it (0 to 5 minutes) on the **Route** tab.

Tap a day on the **Week** tab to see its timeline, one section per run, with **Layover** rows between runs. Green means the rider hears their song aboard. Red means it misses them.

One playlist per day holds every run, in order.

### When the music runs out

Sometimes a run's music ends before the run does. The **Week** tab shows it:

- A grey striped stretch at the end of a day's time bar is time with no music.
- A line under the day says how long and where: "About 12 min with no music at the end of Afternoon Run 1". If the same thing happens on several days, one line near the top covers the week instead.

Tap the line to see why, and fix it there:

- **A Driver's picks run with no picks that can play.** Add driver's picks, import a playlist, or change the run to riders' requests.
- **Keep music going to the end is on, but no pick can play.** Add picks (clean ones while Clean songs only is on).
- **Riders' songs fill less than half of a run, with Keep music going to the end off.** Turn it on, or add songs for more riders.

With Keep music going to the end off, a shorter quiet end is your choice, so only the grey stretch shows.

A line goes away once it's fixed. Lines wait while today's music is playing, and never show in Now Playing. Tap a day to see each gap under its run.

### Tips

Now and then the **Week** tab shows a tip: one thing that would help right now. Tips show only while you're parked, one at a time, about one new tip a week. Tap the button to act on it, or **×** to hide it for good. A tip leaves when it no longer applies.

## What they say

- **Music ran out before your last stop**, **music between runs**, **a late start** and **time for a change of scenery**.
- **A rider has no song on some days.** Add a song and they hear one every day.
- **Test mode is on** before a school morning ends, with **Turn off Test mode**.
- **A rider hasn't been riding for over two weeks:** "Still away?" The two weeks count from when Seats & Beats first notices.
- **Your picks repeat often:** fewer than 8 driver's picks, with **Open Driver's picks**.
- **Already have a playlist?** After three picks added one at a time: **Import a playlist**.
- **Auto sync,** after you tap Sync three days running: **Turn on auto sync**.
- **New school year?** After 60 days with no music: **New school year**.
- **Crossfade,** a week after your first music: **Open Settings**.
- **Tap a song** to see when it plays next: **Got it**.

Recent activity notes when a tip was used or hidden, never which rider it named. A hidden tip stays hidden on your other devices.

### Runs, schools and the layover

A **run** is one trip. On the **Route** tab, tap **+** and follow the steps. Enter each stop once, with both times (or **Skip**). Run times come from your stops.

**The other half.** A pair shows **↔ Afternoon Run 2**; a lone run shows **Morning only** or **Afternoon only**. On its page, **Add the afternoon half…** makes the other trip. A stop added to either run asks for both times. To remove one, pick **Remove from this run** or **Remove from both runs**. If the other half has stops this run lacks, tap **Add it here** or **Not needed**. To reorder runs, tap **⋯**.

- **Music:** **Requests** (riders' songs), **Driver's picks**, or **No music**. A **No music** run leaves your other runs' music as it is; for **Driver's picks**, see **How the plan works**.
- **School stops:** mornings have **Arrive** and **Release**; afternoons **Arrive** and **Leave**, and songs start once you leave.

**Between runs.** With **Pause**, music stops after a run; Now Playing shows **Layover** and Play. **Keep picks playing** plays picks until you tap **Start Run 2**.

**Two runs in the morning.** Riders' songs stop at school after their run; a second run plays what you set. In the afternoon, Play starts the riders' run where the morning stopped. With **Keep music going to the end** on and no music on the second run, picks carry on at school for up to 20 minutes.

### Riders' song lists

Each rider has a list of up to 10 songs (5 to 15 with **Songs per rider** in **Settings**, the gear at the top right, under **Playlist options**). One plays each school day:

- **Every song gets a turn** before any repeats. Ten songs take two weeks.
- **One song a day, at most.**
- **Never the same song two days in a row**, even over a weekend.
- **Same song at most …× a week** still counts. With fewer songs than days, driver's picks fill in.
- **On a full day**, Seats & Beats picks a shorter or shared song before anyone sits out.
- **The week's plan stays the same** unless you change songs or shuffle. The rider's page shows each song's days, like **Tue, Fri**.

**Tap a song** to see when it plays next and last played, or to replace or remove it. **See lyrics in Apple Music** (here, on each search result and in a Songs-tab row's menu) opens it in Music; tap ◀ Seats & Beats to return. A radio edit is listed first and marked.

**A full list.** Adding a song asks which one it replaces, oldest and most-played first; nothing changes until you confirm. To start over, tap **Remove all songs** (it asks first). Lowering **Songs per rider** removes nothing.

**Shuffle this week** on the **Week** tab moves this week's songs to other days; today's stay put.

## Seating

Your bus's seating chart: set it up, keep it right, and print it. Use it while you are parked.

### Seating chart

The **Seats** tab holds your seating chart. Use it parked.

**Set up your bus.** Tap **Set up your bus**:

1. **How big is your bus?** Rows, numbering, and an optional **Bus number** to print on the chart.
2. **How many to a seat?**
3. **Any special seats?** Tap a seat that's smaller, missing, or kept free.
4. **Which runs use this chart?** Add the run and its riders first. Missing one? Tap **Don't see your run?**
5. **Riders' grades.** Skip it if you don't seat by grade.

Change it later in **⋯**, **Chart settings** (**Delete this chart…** asks first).

**Seat your riders.** Drag a rider into a seat, or tap them and choose **Move…**. The rest wait under **Not seated**. When the chart is right, tap **This is my chart**. Then each seat change waits in **⋯**, **Name tags to move**, until you tick it.

**Up front today.** A rider you put up front shows ahead of their seat, tagged **Up front today**. Tomorrow it is back to normal.

**To fix** lists each rule the chart breaks; tap one to re-work. **Could be better** lists unmet wishes.

**More than one chart.** In **Chart settings**, tap **Add another chart**. Pick one at the top; change its runs with **Used for**.

**Your lists** are buttons under the title: your reward, **Incidents**, **Bus cards**, **Reports**. A number means something is waiting: riders suggested for a reward, incidents to write up, riders who need a card.

**New phone?** Charts are in iCloud.

### Connections

A connection tells the chart how two riders should sit, on every chart both are on.

**The four strengths:**

- **Do not separate (≡):** always in the same seat. Groups that must sit together can make the front one row longer.
- **Best friends (++):** the same seat if there is room, otherwise as close as possible.
- **Friends (+):** in the same seat or across the aisle.
- **Keep apart (−):** at least 2 rows between them. Change it in **Chart settings**, **Set up the bus**.

Do not separate and Keep apart are rules: a re-work never breaks them. Move riders by hand into a broken rule and it shows under **To fix**. If a bus has more such groups than seats, the re-work says so: remove a link or free some seats. Best friends and Friends are wishes, and count first when you re-work the chart.

**Add one** from a rider's page: under **Connections**, tap **Add a connection**, pick the other rider, then a strength. Or tap a rider on the chart and choose **Connect to another rider…**. Swipe a connection and tap **Remove**; it asks first.

**Lifting a keep-apart.** Change or remove a **Keep apart** and the app reminds you to check. The next day the Week tab shows **Look again at Ava and Ben**, with **Open the chart**. It shows once more three days later, then stops. Their pages say **Was kept apart · looking again**. Tap **Stop reminding me** to end it sooner.

It shows only while you are parked.

### Re-working a chart

**What starts one.** **Re-work the chart…**, a line under **To fix**, a rider's **Move closer to the front…** or **Move away from here…**, or **Change of scenery…** in **⋯**.

**It never breaks** seat sizes, special seats, front-row riders, Do not separate or Keep apart; if it can't, it says why.

**The proposal** shows the whole bus: seats where nobody arrives or leaves are faded, and riders who move have an arrow. It lists what it keeps and what it **Gives up**. **Try another** shows a different one. **Accept and show the move list** saves it and lists the name tags to move.

**Marks.** A rider with a Not good or follow-up mark in the last 2 weeks isn't seated beside a friend.

**Earlier charts** and **Change of scenery…** appear in **⋯** once you tap **This is my chart**. A copy is saved before each re-work; **Go back to this chart** asks first.

**Change of scenery** gives everyone a new view. Locked riders stay. The others move to a different row if their age group has room, else across the aisle. It weighs, in order: friends together, every seat used, ages in order, boys and girls apart, then new seat-mates. **Lock anyone you placed on purpose, such as helpers seated with younger riders; they stay put.** The Week tab suggests it a month after the chart was made or last used.

Other re-works keep friendships, then grade order, then boys and girls apart, then the fewest moves.

### Name tags to move

If your board has name tags, this keeps the list of the ones to move. Use it parked.

**Where.** On the Seats tab, tap **⋯**, then **Name tags to move**. When any are waiting, a line under the chart's title says how many.

**What's listed.** Every rider whose seat changed since you last dealt with their tag: after a re-work, after going back to an earlier chart, or after a move you made by hand. Each line shows the whole move, by where the tag is on your board, front of the bus first: **Cal: seat 7 → seat 13**, **Ava: seat 9 (new)**, **Ben: seat 12 → not seated**. A rider moved and moved back isn't listed. A rider you turn **Riding this year** off for is, so their tag comes off the board.

**Tick each tag** once you've moved it. It leaves the list, on your other devices too. **All done** ticks the rest, and asks first. Start at the front of the bus and work back.

**When it starts.** The list begins when you tap **This is my chart**, so a new chart starts empty. Each chart has its own list. Seat changes from before this arrived aren't listed.

### Printing the chart

Tap **⋯**, then **Print or share**. Two choices appear. Then Print, Save to Files, Messages and the rest are on the share sheet. They are made for a black-and-white printer, and leave your phone only when you send them.

- **Chart:** the seating chart alone, on one page (a very long bus goes on to a second). Names sit side by side, just as riders sit.
- **Chart with stops and schools:** the same page one, then a rider list on page two with each rider's stop and school. Keep this one in your emergency folder.

**The title** carries your bus number, if you gave one in **Chart settings**, **Set up the bus**. So does the file name.

**Page one** says when it was printed and when the seats last changed. The rider list pages say when it was printed. A copy in a binder shows how current it is. Print a new one when the dates are old.

A rider with no seat is still listed, so no name is left off. If a rider has no stop or school, the list says **no stop** or **no school**. Fix it on their page, then print again.

### Rider notes and marks

**Add marks or a note.** On a rider's page, tap **Add a mark or note**, or tap a rider on the chart. Tap every mark that applies under **Good**, **Not good** or **Needs follow-up**; tap again to clear. Add a **Note**, saved with the first mark shown, and **With another rider** if two were involved. A note alone is fine. Tap **Save**.

**Today.** What you saved today is listed first; tap one to change it, or swipe left to remove it.

**Up front today** lasts the day; turn it off if it was a mistake. **Not riding** is on the after-run checklist.

**On a rider's page.** **Last 2 weeks** counts good rides, not good marks and follow-ups. An open follow-up shows under it until you tap **Done**. **History** lists marks, notes, seat changes, up front days, connections and the watch list. Tap a mark or note to change or **Remove** it (it asks first).

**On my watch list** puts a rider on the **Watch list** chip on the Riders tab. It changes no seat or music.

**Marks and the chart.** A re-work won't seat a rider with a recent Not good or follow-up mark beside a friend.

**Your own marks.** Open **Seats → ⋯ → Chart settings → Rider marks**, or tap **Edit this list**, to add, rename, reorder or remove marks. Marks already given keep their wording.

**Privacy.** Notes and marks stay in your iCloud. A support file shows only that a numbered rider had a mark, never names or your words.

### The after-run checklist

After a run, the **Week** tab asks one question, while you are parked: **How was the Morning run?**

**When it shows.** The run has a seating chart with a rider seated, you drove it, it has ended, and no music is playing. A run with music has ended when its music stopped or finished; a No music run, when its time has passed. The card waits while you drive the next run.

**All good** checks the run in one tap.

**Check riders** lists the run's riders A to Z. Tap **A to Z** above the list to switch to **By seat**, front to back (riders with no seat last). The phone remembers. Mark only what stood out:

- Tap a rider (Ben) to add marks or a note. What you saved shows under the name.
- Marks and notes you saved today are listed first, under **Today**. Tap one to change it, or swipe it left to remove it. Removing asks first.
- **Not riding the Morning run today** means the rider was not aboard, so the run counts neither for nor against them. It changes no music.

Tap **Done** to check the run. Everyone you left alone counts as a good ride. **Cancel** leaves it unchecked and keeps what you saved.

Unchecked runs are forgotten at midnight.

**Up front on 3 days.** After a third day up front in a school week, a card offers **Look at it**: a re-work that moves their seat closer to the front. **×** hides it for that week.

### Marking an incident

**Mark incident** saves the time with one tap; write it up later, parked, and the office can pull camera footage. It needs a seating chart.

## The one tap

- **Now Playing:** **Mark incident**, below the controls.
- **Lock Screen:** the round **Mark** button, which shows a tick and the time.
- **Week tab, a run with no music:** a card with **Mark incident**. **Start run** there (from 15 minutes before) puts a **Mark** button on your Lock Screen. **End run** removes it, as does music starting or 15 minutes after the run ends.

It asks nothing; taps within two minutes are one marker. It goes to the run playing, just paused, or started with Start run, else the no-music run under way.

## Without opening the app

- **Control:** touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap **Customize**, add **Mark incident**. Or use Control Center.
- **Action button:** in **Settings**, **Action Button**, **Shortcut**, **Mark incident**.
- **Siri:** "Mark incident in Seats & Beats".

Each works locked. With no music, stops come from your schedule, and the write-up says so.

## Writing it up

The Week tab shows **1 incident to write up**. Or tap **Incidents** under the **Seats** title; **Add an incident** covers one you missed.

**Needs follow-up** counts against the riders involved: no reward first, and a re-work keeps them from a friend two weeks.

## The summary

**Summary** makes text with the time, four minutes of footage either side, names and your words. **Copy** keeps it ten minutes; **Share…** sends it.

### Reports

Both are PDFs. Tap **Reports** under the **Seats** title (you need a seating chart). A rider's page then has **Share a report** too.

## Principal summary

For one school at a time. No dates, and none of your notes. It lists **Needs attention** (two or more Not good marks, or any follow-up or incident), **Kudos** (a good ride on every run you checked, with at least four checked runs) and **Game changer** (three or more marks or follow-ups in the same length of time before, none since). A rider under Needs attention is in no other list.

It covers the time since you last spoke to that principal: under **Last spoke**, **Choose a day**, or **Clear** to go back to the last 14 days. After sharing, it offers to set it to today.

## One rider's report

Everything about one rider, newest first, over **Last 2 weeks**, **Last 30 days**, **This school year** or **Choose dates**.

- **Notes I wrote** and **Seat changes** can be switched off.
- With **Name other riders** off, a mark naming a second rider reads "with another rider".

**Your notes print exactly as you wrote them. They may name other riders, even with Name other riders off. Read the preview before you share it.** An incident prints as "Incident"; where it happened is left out. A note prints up to 2,000 characters.

## Where it goes

The share sheet doesn't offer Copy, and the file is removed when it closes. A report leaves your iPhone only when you share it.

### Weekly rewards

Suggests who earned a reward each week. Off until you turn it on; it needs a seating chart.

## Turning it on

On the **Seats** tab, tap your reward's button under the title. Set **Called**, **How many a week**, **Most for one rider in a month** and **Schools**.

## The weekly card

On weekdays, while parked, the Week tab shows a card with **Choose riders**. It goes once you give them; tap **×** to hide it until next week.

## The list

It covers last week: a point for each good ride (from runs you checked), two for each Good mark. The top scores are **Suggested**, ticked (unless nothing was recorded); ties go to whoever waited longest. The other groups start unticked. With no runs checked, Good marks decide.

**Everyone else** holds the rest, and why: at the monthly most, or nothing recorded. The limits only steer suggestions: tick anyone.

**Tick all** ticks everyone; **Untick all** clears it. Nothing is saved until you tap **Give 12 rewards**.

**Given this week** shows who got one, and the day. Swipe left to remove one (it asks first); they return to the list.

## One rider

On a rider's page, under Notes, **Give a reward** gives one now, outside the weekly list. It counts toward the monthly most only. Remove it in History.

## Earlier weeks

At the bottom, see who got one in each of the last 12 weeks, and swipe left to remove one. Gave some before using this list? Tick them and tap **Add**.

### Bus cards

Keeps track of which riders need a bus card, so the details are ready for your district's form.

## The list

On the **Seats** tab, tap **Bus cards** under the title (you need a seating chart). Riders sit under **Needs a card**, **Requested** and **Handed out**. Tap **+** to add a rider who needs one. The button on a row moves the rider on: **Requested**, **Handed out**, then **Clear**, which asks first. A rider with no last name shows "add last name".

A rider's page has a **Bus card** row under About. It opens the same screen as a row in the list.

## The details

Enter the **Last name** and **Student number** (both optional). Tap **Copy** beside the first name, last name, student number or school, or **Copy all**, then paste into the form. Copy keeps the details on this iPhone for ten minutes. Each change of state shows in the rider's History.

## The district's form

Tap **Add the address** and paste your form's web address; it must start with http or https. To get it from a QR code, open the code with the Camera app and copy the address from Safari. **Open the district's form** then opens it in Safari.

Seats & Beats never fills in the form and sends nothing. Last names, student numbers and the address stay in your own iCloud and follow you to your other devices. A support file shows only each numbered rider's card state.

## Driving day

What to do each morning and afternoon. Once the music is playing, you should not need to touch the phone.

### Pressing play

The plan works when music starts on time.

- **Morning:** at your run's **Press play** time, tap the yellow card on the **Week** tab, such as **PLAY MONDAY MORNING**.
- **One run each way:** pause at school. Once the morning's songs are done, or 15 minutes after its planned end, the music stops. If the songs finish early, driver's picks carry on for up to 20 minutes. Tap **PLAY AFTERNOON RUN** as you leave, even a few minutes early: the afternoon is timed from it.
- **More runs:** with **Between runs** set to **Pause**, the music stops at the end of each run; tap **Play** for the next one. With **Keep picks playing**, picks play until you tap **Start Run 2**. See **Runs, schools and the layover**.

**Which run.** With more than one run, a chooser under the yellow card (**AM 1 | AM 2 | PM 1**) picks the run by the clock; tap another to start there. Each run is timed from its Play. Before the afternoon, the button says when it starts; pick that run to start early.

**Slow roads?** Pick **Slow +10%** or **Slow +20%** under the yellow card before pressing play; it resets daily.

**Late?** Up to 45 minutes late, the timing moves with your press. If your first Play comes after the morning run was due to end, the app asks which run you're starting, while parked. See **Late starts and missed mornings**.

**Trying it out?** Use **Start over** in **⋯**, or **Test mode**, so practice plays don't count.

### Playing music on the bus

Apple Music subscriptions are for personal use. Before you play music for students, check your school district's or employer's policy on music on the bus.

Some districts have rules or licenses for this. Whether to play music, and what kind, is your decision and your district's. Seats & Beats cannot license music for public play.

This is not legal advice. If you are not sure, ask your transportation office.

### Now Playing

**Now Playing** shows the song in big letters, with the rider's name small underneath, and what's next. Nothing needs a tap.

**Mini-player bar.** After **Done**, a slim bar above the tabs has play, pause and skip.

**Lock screen.** On iOS 18 or later, a card and the Dynamic Island show whose song it is. They clear at school or after 15 minutes paused.

**Mark incident.** With a seating chart, a button below the controls saves the time of an incident in one tap; the Lock Screen card has a round **Mark** button. See **Marking an incident**. With GPS on, **Mark stop** shows under it while the run has a stop GPS hasn't found: tap it once, stopped at that stop, and confirm which stop it was on the Week tab once you're parked. It asks nothing.

**Layover.** After a run, it shows **Layover · Run 2 starts 7:55** and a big **Play** button. With **Keep picks playing** (**Between runs**, Route tab), picks play instead, with **Start Run 2**.

**Skip.** A song skipped well before its end counts as **not heard**, so that rider goes first next time. A run's last song can't be skipped.

**When songs run out.** With **Keep music going to the end** on, your driver's picks carry on until you pause. The afternoon's songs wait for your Play.

**Crossfade.** Set it in **Settings**, under **Playlist options**; the Music app's setting doesn't apply.

**If something goes wrong,** a yellow box says what to do. If the app closed, reopen it and tap **RESUME**.

**The ⋯ menu:** **End today's music** asks first (to stop briefly, Pause). **Start over** forgets today's plays.

For a delay day, see **Late starts and missed mornings**.

### Late starts and missed mornings

**A late start** (such as a 2-hour delay) moves every morning run later by the same amount. The afternoon never moves.

**Set it before you press Play.** Under the yellow card on the **Week** tab, pick **Late start today**: None, 1 hr, 2 hr, or Other, for today only.

**If you forget, the app asks.** If the day's first Play comes after your riders' morning run was due to end, with no late start set, you see **Which run are you starting?**:

- **Running late: start Run 1** moves the morning's times by what the button says and starts that run.
- **Not a late start** is for an ordinary day. Nothing plays now. Usually the afternoon takes this morning's songs where there's room, and the Play card says **Not started this morning**. With **Keep music going to the end** off, they aren't moved. With driver's picks on a later morning run, this button is **Start Run 2** and says how many riders go without a song.
- **Cancel** changes nothing.

It never asks if you set **Late start today** first (None counts) or the day's music has started. A run you pick in the chooser and start after its end counts as a late start, unasked. Once the morning's music starts, the late start can't be changed.

**A morning you don't drive.** Press Play in the afternoon. Riders whose song was planned for the morning are heard then if there's room; morning-only riders go first another day.

## How fairness works

When there is more music than ride, someone has to wait. Seats & Beats makes sure it is never the same rider every time.

### Fairness

Sometimes the songs add up to more time than a run. When that happens, some riders **sit out** that day. They are not forgotten.

- **Who shares the time:** a morning run and the afternoon run it is joined to are planned as one list, so their riders share that time. Any other run is planned on its own. A **Driver's picks** or **No music** run has no rotation.
- **Who sits out:** Seats & Beats tracks who was left out over the last 4 weeks. When there is not room, riders left out the least sit out first. Riders left out the most are protected.
- **Who goes first next time:** A rider who sat out goes first next time.
- **Sitting out list:** On a day's page, look for **Sitting out today**. Their songs wait at the end of the playlist.
- **The weekly limit:** In **Settings** (the gear at the top right), **Same song at most 2× a week** stops one song from filling every day.

**Sitting out** is different from **Misses it**. Sitting out means the song was left off that day to make room. Misses it means the song plays, but not while the rider is aboard.

For a rider's history, open their page on the **Riders** tab and scroll to **Fairness**. A filled check means heard. A filled X means not heard. An outlined check means planned. A clock means planned to sit out. A dash means no song that day.

Pausing a rider never counts against them.

## Options

Everything you can change to fit your bus.

### Driver's picks

Driver's picks are your own songs. They play when no rider has a song waiting, keep each run's music going to its end, and fill **Driver's picks** runs and the layover with **Keep picks playing**.

**Play driver's picks.** For a trip Seats & Beats doesn't plan, tap **Play driver's picks** under the yellow card on the **Week** tab when no run is playing. While **Keep music going to the end** is on, they also carry on by themselves when riders' songs run out: after the day's last song, and on a late morning. **Pause** stops them for now; **End driver's picks** in the ⋯ menu clears them (paused picks clear after 15 minutes). A run's **Play** takes over.

On the **Songs** tab, tap **Driver's picks**, then **+**. Swipe right (or press and hold) for **Lyrics** in Apple Music. To remove one, swipe left and tap **Remove**. Under **⋯**, then **Edit**, drag to reorder, or select picks (or **Select all**) and tap **Remove**.

Picks play least played first and fill room between riders' songs. Picks never take a rider's turn. A pick that's also a rider's song that day sits out.

To import picks, tap **⋯**, then **Import a playlist**, with **Goes into** set to **Driver's picks**, then **Add to my picks** or **Replace my current picks**. A song that's already a pick is skipped; **Remove duplicates** cleans up the rest.

### Driver's picks settings

Three settings in **Settings** (the gear at the top right), under **Playlist options** decide when your picks play:

- **Fill gaps with driver's picks:** before a pickup, a pick plays instead of a rider's song that would start before they board. Example: Play at 6:50, first pickup 6:58: two picks, then that rider's song.
- **Keep music going to the end:** after the riders' songs, picks play until the run ends. Off, no picks are added at the end.
- **Driver's picks**, just below it: **Mixed in** or **After riders' songs** decides where those end picks go. It's greyed out while Keep music is off; your choice is kept.

**Mixed in** (the default) spreads picks between riders' songs when there's room. Example: everyone boards at school, first stop 15 minutes away: song, pick, song, pick, song. On a full bus there's little room, so most picks still play at the end.

**After riders' songs:** song, song, song, then picks.

**Riders come first.** Mixing in never changes who hears their song. A rider's song moves later only if it still ends at least 4 minutes before their stop or school release. Picks stay on their own run.

**Least played first.** The picks played least in your Bus playlists over the last four weeks come up first. Ties are shuffled each day.

After an update that changes this order, your Bus playlists are rewritten once.

### Pausing a rider

Pause a rider when they will be away, for a trip, illness or suspension. Their songs come off the playlists, and it does not count as being left out.

To pause:

- On the **Riders** tab, swipe left on the rider and tap **Pause**.
- Or open the rider's page, scroll to **Status** at the bottom, and tap **Pause songs**.

Choose **Rest of this week**, **1 week**, **2 weeks** or **Until I turn it back on**.

To bring them back, swipe and tap **Resume**, or tap **Resume songs** on their page.

After you pause or resume, the Apple Music playlists update by themselves. If you sync by hand, tap **Sync** on the **Week** tab.

If a rider has stopped riding for the year, turn off **Riding this year** in **Status** instead. They move to **Not riding this year** at the bottom of the Riders list and can come back later.

If they are gone for good, tap **Remove rider**, the last button on their page on the Riders tab. It deletes the rider and their songs, so it asks first.

### Shared songs

Two riders sometimes ask for the same song. When you add it, Seats & Beats tells you, with a message like "Maya already has this song." Tap **Add it** to add it anyway, or **Choose a different song**.

A shared song can play once for both riders while they're both on the bus. On a full day, Seats & Beats lines it up on the same day for both. That saves music time, which leaves room for other riders. A shared song shows a **Shared** tag on the day's page, with both names.

Sharing only works if the two riders are on the bus together for the whole song. If their rides overlap by less than the song's length, each rider gets a separate play instead.

If one rider has the clean version and the other has the explicit one, both use the clean version.

A song can't be on the same rider's list twice. If you pick one that's already there, Seats & Beats says so.

### Clean and long songs

**Clean songs only** is on when you install Seats & Beats. Explicit songs (marked with a small **E**) cannot be chosen, and they will not play. If a rider's song is explicit, tap **Switch to the clean version** on their page. Seats & Beats looks for the clean twin on Apple Music.

To check a song's words, tap **See lyrics in Apple Music** under a search result. It opens that exact version in Music, where the lyrics button is. "No lyrics in Apple Music" means there are none; "This song isn't on Apple Music any more" means it's gone.

To allow explicit songs, go to **Settings** (the gear at the top right), then **Playlist options**, and turn off **Clean songs only**.

**Long songs** use up a lot of the ride, and leave less room for other riders. Seats & Beats warns you with an orange note, "Longer than 5 minutes", when you pick one.

In **Settings**, under **Playlist options**, **Flag long songs** turns the warning on or off. While it's on, **Longer than 5 min** sets the cutoff, from 3 to 10 minutes.

The warning is only a heads-up. The song still plays. If the day gets too crowded, a rider may sit out. Ask for a shorter song if that keeps happening.

### Sync and playlists

Seats & Beats builds playlists in Apple Music: **Bus – Monday** through **Bus – Friday**, plus **Bus – Driver's Picks**. Each day's playlist holds every run, in order. Riders' songs take turns, so they change every week.

**They stay up to date by themselves.** Shortly after you make changes, and when you leave the app, the playlists that changed are updated. While music plays, it waits. Your other playlists are never touched.

The **Week** tab shows one line, like **Playlists up to date**. Tap it for details and **Sync now**. A Bus playlist edited in the Music app is put right.

**A card appears only when you need to act:** Apple Music isn't connected, a sync failed, or a Bus playlist was deleted. It says what to tap.

To sync by hand, turn off **Keep playlists up to date automatically** in **Settings** (the gear at the top right), under **Playlist options**, then tap **Sync** on **Week**. Sample riders pause it until you remove them in **Settings**.

Playlists from when the app was called Bus DJ are still used. You won't get second copies.

**On a new iPhone**, syncing waits for your data from iCloud and reuses the Bus playlists already there. If it says **Bus playlists not found** and you deleted them, tap it, then **Sync now**.

**Already have playlists?** Tap **⋯** on the **Songs** tab, then **Import a playlist**. **Test mode** (in **Settings**) writes **TEST Bus –** playlists.

### Save cellular data

Seats & Beats streams songs from Apple Music, which uses cellular data on the bus. Downloaded songs play from your iPhone instead.

**Do this once:** in the Music app, open each playlist, **Bus – Monday** through **Bus – Friday** and **Bus – Driver's Picks**, and tap **Download**.

Seats & Beats then plays the downloaded songs. When the playlists update, new songs download automatically. Songs that aren't downloaded yet still stream.

**Wi-Fi or cellular?** By default, the Music app downloads only on Wi-Fi. Make changes at home or at school, where there's Wi-Fi, and the new songs are ready before you drive. To download over cellular too, turn on **Downloads** in **Settings → Apps → Music → Cellular Data**.

Using **Test mode**? Download the **TEST Bus** playlists too. Seats & Beats plays those while test mode is on.

If a downloaded song won't play, Seats & Beats streams it instead. You don't need to do anything.

### Playlist covers

Apple does not let apps set playlist pictures, so Seats & Beats makes them: one for each weekday, and one for Driver's Picks. Each cover shows the logo and the day. You only do this once. Seats & Beats keeps updating the same playlists, so the covers stay.

1. Go to **Settings** (the gear at the top right), then **Playlist covers**.
2. Tap **Save all six to Photos**. If asked, let Seats & Beats add to Photos.
3. Open the Music app, then **Library**, then **Playlists**, then **Bus – Monday**.
4. Tap **⋯**, then **Edit**.
5. Tap the cover, then **Choose Photo**, and pick the MON cover.
6. Tap **Done**, then repeat for Tuesday through Friday, and **Bus – Driver's Picks** with the PICKS cover.

If saving fails, the screen says why. If Seats & Beats can't add to Photos, tap **Open Settings** there, then **Photos**, and allow adding photos.

### New school year

At the start of the year, clear last year's requests.

Go to **Settings** (the gear at the top right), scroll to **Reset**, and tap **Start a new school year…**. Then choose:

- **Clear songs, keep riders:** removes every song request, pause and history. Riders stay, with their school, seats and connections. You can add new songs.
- **Clear songs and remove all riders:** starts your rider list fresh too. Their seats, connections and earlier charts go with them.

Your runs, stops, schools, driver's picks and seating charts always stay. This cannot be undone.

Afterwards:

- Update your runs, times and schools on the **Route** tab if they changed.
- Add or fix riders and stops.
- Pick songs. The playlists update by themselves.

**Erase all data**, just below, is different. It deletes everything, including your runs and stops. Use it only if you want to start over completely.

## Backup

Your riders and songs are saved for you, so a lost or new phone is not a disaster.

### iCloud backup

Seats & Beats saves your riders, runs, stops, songs, seating charts, connections and earlier charts to your own private iCloud. Nobody else can see them.

Check **iCloud backup** at the top of **Settings** (the gear at the top right). A red **!** on the gear means something needs you.

- **Backed up:** all good.
- **Backing up… / Waiting to back up / Checking iCloud…:** wait a minute. A change waits until you're connected.
- **Not signed in to iCloud:** open the iPhone's own **Settings** app and sign in. Until then, your riders are only on this phone.
- **iCloud is off for Seats & Beats:** open the iPhone's **Settings** app, tap your name, then **iCloud**, and turn on Seats & Beats.
- **iCloud storage is full:** free up or buy storage.
- **iCloud couldn't save the latest changes:** Seats & Beats retries by itself.
- **iCloud backup is paused: Seats & Beats needs an update:** your riders are safe. Install the update once it's out.

**Settings that follow you.** Crossfade, auto sync, **Between runs**, **Driver's picks** and **Songs per rider** follow you to your other devices on the same Apple Account.

**Two devices on different versions?**

- **Seats & Beats is newer on another of your devices:** update this one. Until then it plays but leaves your playlists alone. Not using that device? Tap **I don't use that device anymore**.
- **Another of your devices is running an older Seats & Beats:** update Seats & Beats there, then sync.

### Recent activity

Open **Settings** and tap **Recent activity** to see what changed and what played over the last 14 days, one plain sentence at a time, newest day first.

**The filters.** **All** shows everything. **Changes** is what you edited: riders, songs, stops, runs, seating and settings. **Music** is what played. **Sync** is playlist updates and your iCloud backup.

Marks, notes and checked runs show too, and so do incidents, reports, rewards, bus cards, tips and the GPS test. They say that something happened, never a name or your words.

**A run is one line.** Tap it to see the run song by song: when you pressed Play, whose song played, any skip or pause, and how the run ended. Times are when each song started on this iPhone.

**Names are today's names.** Recent activity keeps no names. It looks each one up when you open the screen, so a rider, stop or run you have since removed shows as "no longer here". It can say that a name changed, not what it was before. A song's title shows only while the song is still in a rider's list or your driver's picks.

**It stays on this iPhone.** It keeps 14 days, and Erase all data clears it. It only leaves the phone inside a support file you choose to share.

**When to look.** If something seems off, like a rider who didn't hear their song, look here first. If it doesn't explain it, share a support file.

### Sharing a support file

If something isn't working, the person helping you may ask to see your setup. Open **Settings** and tap **Share a support file**.

**What's in it.** Your runs, stops and schools with their times. Each rider's grade, boy or girl, stop, school and songs (as song numbers). The driver's picks, the last 8 weeks of what played, and your seating charts and connections. It shows when a numbered rider got a mark, was up front or not riding, never a mark's name or your words. Incidents, rewards and bus card states appear the same way, and a GPS test drive as numbers only. With GPS on it says how many stops of each run have a location, never where one is.

**Recent activity, and this iPhone.** The last 14 days of Recent activity, with no names; this iPhone's model, iOS version, free storage in round steps and settings such as Low Power Mode; your app settings; and whether Apple Music and iCloud are working.

**What's never in it.** Nothing you typed: no names, student numbers, notes, reward's name, form address, names of marks or bus number. No location, no Apple ID, no iPhone name. Riders show as "Rider 7", stops as "Stop 4".

**It has no names, but it isn't secret.** Someone who knows your route could work out who a rider is from a grade and a stop. Share it only with the person helping you. Nothing is sent unless you share it, and Seats & Beats keeps no copy.

**Who is who.** On this screen, **Who is who** turns "Rider 7" back into a name. It stays on your iPhone.

### GPS

Optional, and off unless you turn it on. With GPS on, Seats & Beats learns where your stops are and when the bus really gets there. It changes nothing about your music.

## Turning it on

Parked, with your music paused or stopped, open **Settings**, then **GPS**, and turn on **Use GPS**. When iPhone asks, choose **While Using the App**. Learning starts with your next run's music.

## Learning your stops

Drive each run as usual. A stop is found once two drives stop in the same place. Parked afterwards, the Week tab says how many are found so far: **Save** keeps them; **Review** opens the run's **Stop locations** (Route tab, a run).

For a stop it doesn't find, tap **Mark stop** on Now Playing while stopped there. Parked, the Week tab shows your mark with its best guess at the stop: **Save** or **Discard**. There is no map, and no location is ever shown.

## Real stop times

After a few days, a stop that is usually 2 minutes or more early or late is offered a new time. You tick the ones you want; nothing changes by itself.

## What it keeps

Stop locations and arrival times are saved on this iPhone and in your iPhone's own backup. They are never sent, and never in a support file. Nothing is tracked when music is not playing, so runs with no music are not learned. Turning GPS off keeps your locations; **Forget all locations** removes them.

### GPS test drive

An optional one-day test of how well this iPhone can tell when the bus is at a stop. It is off unless you turn it on, and Seats & Beats works the same without it.

## Running it

1. Parked, with your music stopped, open **Settings**, then **GPS**, then **GPS test drive**. (The page is there while a test is on or has a result.)
2. Turn on **Test my next day of music**. When iPhone asks about your location, choose **While Using the App**. With "Allow Once" the test turns itself off when your music starts, and says why.
3. Drive as usual. The test covers the next day a planned run's music plays, then switches itself off.

It measures only while a run's music is actually playing: not while paused, not driver's picks outside a run, not runs with no music. It changes nothing about your music. If Seats & Beats is closed during a run, that run's numbers are lost. iPhone shows a blue marker at the top while it measures.

## What it keeps

Times and measurements only: which scheduled stops (school stops too) it noticed, other standstills such as lights, how exact the position was, the battery, and **Kept working while locked**: Yes, No, or "Not checked: the phone stayed unlocked". Where you were is never saved or sent.

## The result

It shows on the same page. **Share a support file** sends the numbers and no locations. **Run the test again** replaces the result, and **Remove this result…** removes it; both ask first. The switch and result stay on this iPhone.

### Privacy

Seats & Beats collects nothing about you or your riders.

- **Where your data lives.** Riders, stops, songs, seating charts, marks, notes and play history stay on this iPhone and in your own private iCloud. The developer cannot see them.
- **What leaves your iPhone.** A support file, a report, a seating chart or an incident summary leaves your iPhone only when you share or copy it. A support file has no names.
- **Rider notes.** Marks, notes, incidents, rewards and bus card details are yours alone, in your own iCloud. A report or an incident summary has names and your words in it. **Copy** keeps text on this iPhone only, for ten minutes.
- **Recent activity.** A record of what happened in the app for 14 days, with no names, sent only inside a support file you share.
- **No ads, no analytics, no tracking.** Nothing is sent to advertisers or anyone else.
- **Apple Music.** Used only to search for songs, play and preview them, and keep the bus playlists up to date.
- **Location.** Seats & Beats uses your location only if you turn on GPS (or a GPS test drive), and then only while a run's music plays (including the one reading **Mark stop** keeps when you tap it). With GPS on it keeps where your stops are and when the bus reached them, saved on this iPhone and in your iPhone's own backup. Nothing is sent anywhere, and a support file has no locations in it.

You can erase everything any time in **Settings** (the gear at the top right), under **Reset**.

## Troubleshooting

Find the symptom, then follow the fix. If the fix does not work, check the internet connection first.

### Something's wrong

**Not sure what happened?** Look in **Settings**, then **Recent activity**, first. If it doesn't explain it, share a support file.

**A song won't play.** Check the internet and that Apple Music is connected and subscribed. Then tap play again. If a rider's page says "No longer on Apple Music", pick another song.

**A rider shows "Misses it."** Tap that day on the **Week** tab. Their song plays, but not while they're aboard. Open their page, pick a shorter song, or check their stop times on the **Route** tab.

**Playlists didn't update.** On the **Week** tab, tap the playlists line, then **Sync now**. If a card says **Retry**, check the internet and tap it.

**A new phone shows nothing yet.** Wait a minute, on Wi-Fi. Make sure you are signed in to the same iCloud and Seats & Beats is turned on there.

**Apple Music isn't connected.** Tap **Connect** on the **Week** tab. If it says the access is off, tap **Open Settings** and allow **Media & Apple Music**. You also need an Apple Music subscription.

**The music stopped when I closed the app.** Reopen Seats & Beats and tap **RESUME**. It continues from the saved song.

**A tip appeared on the Week tab.** Now and then Seats & Beats points out something that would help, like playing driver's picks between runs. Tap its button to act on it, or **×** to hide that tip for good. Tips never show while music is playing. See **Tips**.
