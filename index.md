# Seats & Beats

Seats & Beats is an iPhone app for school bus drivers. It keeps a seating chart for the bus, and plays each rider's own song during the ride through Apple Music.

Only the driver uses the app. Riders do not have accounts, and what a driver enters about riders stays on the driver's iPhone and in the driver's own private iCloud.
