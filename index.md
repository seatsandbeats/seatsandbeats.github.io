# Seats & Beats

Seats & Beats is an iPhone app for school bus drivers. It keeps your seating chart, your notes about each ride, and your riders' song requests in one place. It works with Apple Music.

Only the driver uses the app. Riders do not have accounts, and what a driver enters about riders stays on the driver's iPhone and in the driver's own private iCloud.
